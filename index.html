About Us

Our Background

MADECAF is a coffee sourcing business concept focused on introducing Robusta green coffee from Madagascar to Malaysian coffee businesses. Through a digital platform, we aim to connect coffee origin with professional buyers looking to explore potential sourcing opportunities.

Our Mission

To make Malagasy Robusta green coffee more accessible to Malaysian coffee businesses through product discovery, sample options and direct business enquiries.

Our Vision

To build a recognised connection between Madagascar's coffee origin and international coffee businesses.

Target Customers

• Malaysian coffee roasters seeking green coffee beans
• Independent roaster-cafés exploring potential coffee sourcing options
• Professional coffee buyers interested in discovering coffee from Madagascar

Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

MadeCaf combines a clear geographical identity with a sample-first approach to coffee discovery. Buyers can explore the available products, request samples and contact us to discuss their requirements before considering a larger order.

Privacy Policy

At MADECAF, we respect your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and protect your data:

  • Data Collection: We only collect contact information (names, emails) provided willingly through our contact and sample request forms.
  • Cart Persistence: Shopping cart items are stored strictly in your browser’s local storage (localStorage) and are never sold to third parties.
  • Cookies & Tracking: We do not use third-party tracking pixels or behavioral ad trackers.
  • User Rights: You can request full data deletion at any time by contacting us at sourcing@madecaf.com.

Terms of Service & Sample Shipping (CGV)

General Terms and Conditions governing green coffee sample requests on MADECAF:

  • Sample Dispatch: Green coffee samples (1kg or 5kg) are dispatched via air courier with grain moisture documentation.
  • International Delivery: Standard courier delivery to Malaysia typically takes 4–8 business days.
  • Commercial Orders: Full container or pallet shipments are governed by separate bilateral B2B export contracts.

Returns & Replacement Policy

Our commitments regarding sample shipments:

  • Damaged Samples: If sample packaging arrives torn or moisture compromised, replacement sample pouches are dispatched immediately.
  • Cupping Discrepancies: Comprehensive technical cupping sheets accompany each batch to ensure sample-to-lot consistency.
