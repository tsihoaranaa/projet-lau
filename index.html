Our Background

A Digital Platform Linking Origin & Buyers

MADECAF is a coffee sourcing business concept focused on introducing Robusta green coffee from Madagascar to Malaysian coffee businesses. Through a digital platform, we aim to connect coffee origin with professional buyers looking to explore potential sourcing opportunities.

Privacy Policy

At MADECAF, data confidentiality is fundamental to our B2B roaster partnerships:

  • Data Collection: We only collect contact details willingly submitted for green coffee inquiries and cupping sample dispatch.
  • Local Cart Storage: Selected sample items are securely stored on your browser via `localStorage` and are never monetized.
  • No Invasive Trackers: We don't utilize third-party trackers or cross-site advertising networks.

Terms of Service & Sample Shipping (CGV)

General conditions for green coffee sample evaluations:

  • Sample Dispatch: Sample pouches (1kg / 5kg) are air-shipped with moisture content certificates.
  • Delivery Times: Standard air express delivery to Malaysia arrives in 4–8 business days.
  • Commercial Contracts: 60kg wholesale jute bags and container lots are handled under direct bilateral agreements.

Replacements & Cupping Guarantee

Sample guarantee commitments:

  • Damaged Shipments: Should an air sample pouch arrive damaged, a replacement is dispatched at zero cost.
  • Lot Consistency: Cupping scorecards and moisture logs accompany every lot to ensure alignment with production quantities.
